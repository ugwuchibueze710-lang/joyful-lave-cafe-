Our Story

The beginning.

As a young 16-year-old looking for a job, Ashley Webb — born and raised in Owensboro — was hired at the local coffee shop, Beyond the Brim. Little did she know that her passion and JOY would be found in this industry, finding love for local businesses.

Fast forward almost 20 years and dreams are coming true. She owns and operates a cooking class and meal prep business, Joy’s Cooking Classes and Joy-Full Meal Prep — and the dream of a coffee shop and cafe came to life with Joy-Full Java + Juice.

Our goal is to bring you a cup of JOY for your day as an escape from the world around you.

Our coffee and espresso is sourced from a local roaster, Mylke. We love to use ingredients from local farmers and suppliers, providing the freshest ingredients, made with LOVE!
